Arham Jain

Detection Engineer at FICO

I am Arham Jain, a cybersecurity researcher and Security Operations Analyst passionate about building technologies that help defenders stay ahead of attackers. My work focuses on cloud security, deception engineering, AI-driven security, and threat detection, with an emphasis on developing practical solutions to modern security challenges.

Beyond research, I enjoy mentoring aspiring cybersecurity professionals, contributing to open-source projects, and sharing knowledge through technical writing and community initiatives.

I believe cybersecurity is not just about detecting attacks, it's about understanding adversaries, anticipating their next move, and building defenses that make every attack a source of intelligence.

I'm Devanshu Nagpal, a Cyber Security Engineer (Red Team) at FICO, working on cloud-native red teaming, Kubernetes/EKS security, and AWS identity exploitation. I care deeply about bridging offensive security and defensive cloud engineering, and I am always learning as I go.
