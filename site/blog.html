A deception and detection integration framework, built inside AWS

Decoys and honeypots usually sit at the attack surface. This research plants them inside the environment instead, to definitively confirm an active compromise. It detects adversaries not through signatures, but through their curiosity and mistakes.

  • Honey IAM roles & tokens
  • Honey AWS account
  • Honey artifacts: services, S3 buckets, files
  • SIEM integration with true-positive alerts
